# playerbot autonomy

## Introduction
Guilds form the backbone of any wow server. The ongoing rivalry between guilds and the inate competition is what keeps many players going. So my idea here is to try and recreate that same idea but with bot run and controlled guilds.

## Guild activities

- Crafting and gathering
- Dungeon runs
- Raids
- Duels
- Battlegrounds
- Arenas
- City sieges
- Town sieges
- Inn parties


## Internal guild activities

- Member management
- Internal trading
- Guild bank management
- Resource allocation
- Funding new guild bank tabs
- Recruitment and growth
